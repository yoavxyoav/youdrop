YouTube → AirDrop

Sends the video you are watching, at its current timestamp, to your phone.

Helper token

The token lives in ~/.youtube-airdrop-token.

How to send

  1. Play a YouTube video and pause at the moment you want.
  2. Press ⌘ ⇧ Y, or click the toolbar icon.
  3. The macOS AirDrop picker opens — click your phone in it.
  4. Accept on the phone. The link opens the YouTube app at that timestamp.

Why a helper is needed

Chrome on macOS cannot reach AirDrop. navigator.share exists there but always rejects with NotAllowedError, even with a real click, and no extension API exposes the share sheet. The local helper calls AppKit's documented sharing API instead. macOS deliberately offers no way to preselect the recipient, so the final click is always yours.
